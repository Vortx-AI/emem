Solutions · EUDR due diligence

EUDR evidence, plot by plot.

Check each plot against the forest map the Commission's guidance points to (JRC GFC2020) and against forest loss after 31 December 2020. Each plot gets a signed result that your due-diligence statement and your auditor can check.

Run one plot Talk to us

Key dates

Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 applies, large and medium operators
30 December 2026
Applies, micro and small operators
30 June 2027
Deforestation cut-off
31 December 2020

How a check works

  1. Send the plot. A GeoJSON polygon, or a point for a plot of 4 ha or less that is not cattle land, with the country of production, the commodity code and the quantity.
  2. One read of the whole plot. emem reads every 10 m pixel of the JRC GFC2020 map inside the plot, and the Hansen and TMF pixels that contain it. Each class's area comes from the share of each pixel the plot covers.
  3. A verdict per plot. Deforestation-free; review, when loss falls on land JRC does not map as forest, which is not fileable without your review; or not deforestation-free.
  4. A signed record. One signed fact per plot and an eudr_compliance@2 card. Season, imagery and burn sections can be added to the same answer.

What it gives an auditor

  • The source layers and their versions, and the rule that was applied.
  • The plot's area in each class, so the verdict can be recomputed from the numbers.
  • A plot id that is a salted commitment: the record proves which plot was checked without publishing where it is.
  • A receipt that verifies offline, or in the browser at /verify, without trusting emem.

What it checks and what it does not

It checks Article 3(a), deforestation-free, only. Legality, tenure and free, prior and informed consent are not checked, and every result says so. Legal interpretation, the wider risk assessment and filing the statement stay with the operator and its advisers.

Run one plot

One request, no key. The plot below is made up for the example.

curl -s https://emem.dev/v1/eudr_dds -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{
  "plots": [{
    "plot_id": "example-plot-1",
    "geometry_geojson": {"type": "Polygon", "coordinates": [[[-60.500, -3.500], [-60.498, -3.500], [-60.498, -3.498], [-60.500, -3.498], [-60.500, -3.500]]]},
    "country_of_production": "BRA", "commodity_hs": "0901", "commodity_name": "coffee", "quantity_kg": 12000
  }],
  "operator": {"name": "Example Importer BV", "eori": "NL000000000"}
}'

The same check runs live in the browser at /demos/eudr, and from any MCP host as emem_eudr_dds. Every field is in the reference.
