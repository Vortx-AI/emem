Solutions · agriculture

Crop intelligence for every field, area and district.

Season curves, field boundaries with pixel support, crop timing, burning and water use. Each number names its scenes and how many pixels it rests on.

Talk to us See the API

Three levels, one record

  • FarmOne call per field per season. A full kharif season for a 4.4 ha field came back in 4.2 s: 13 dates, each with the spread of its 437 pixels at 10 m and the share of the field that was clear.
  • AreaFarms within a radius read in the same way, so a 3 to 10 km view is built from the same signed field records, not from a separate model.
  • DistrictTotals from a sample design with an interval, not from summed pixels. A district number states what its interval covers.

Field boundaries: pick the method by the job

  1. Open data at 10 m. Fields of The World outlines with Sentinel-2, cleaned and regularised. No GPU and no imagery licence. Right for area and district work; not the farm unit where fields are small.
  2. About 0.5 m imagery. A field model with edge refinement. A quarter of the pixels of the next option.
  3. About 0.27 m imagery. The sharpest edges, for smallholder plots, with our boundary partners.

Compare them on the same held-out squares, by field size, before choosing. GPU time, imagery licence and the date of the imagery matter as much as the score.

What we say plainly

  • A field under about 0.1 ha has too few 10 m pixels for a field value. We label it and do not guess.
  • Cloud leaves gaps in the monsoon season. A date is given with the gap around it, and radar fills what it can.
  • District totals need a reference sample on the ground. We help design it.
