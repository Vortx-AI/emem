Pick a place. emem resolves it to a cell64, reads the cell's geospatial foundation-model embedding (Tessera, a 128-D annual fingerprint learned from Sentinel-1 SAR + Sentinel-2 optical), and returns the K nearest cells on Earth by cosine similarity, each one a signed fact you can recall and verify. One call: POST /v1/find_similar. Neighbours mean "the surface looks like this here", not "the climate matches", so vintage-to-vintage change is cross-checked separately by triple_consensus below.

POST /v1/find_similar

Multi-encoder consensus at the query cell

Similarity is one encoder's opinion. The headline differentiator is agreement across encoders: POST /v1/triple_consensus recalls the query cell through Clay, Prithvi, and Tessera, measures each encoder's vintage-to-vintage change as 1 - cos(v_now, v_prev), and reports whether they agree that something changed.
