Ask the Earth.
Live calls, signed answers.

Real-world questions an agent might pass to emem.dev, each wired to a live endpoint. Hit run all and watch the responder answer in order. Every answer carries a fact CID and the responder pubkey signature; nothing is mocked. This page is also our test rig. It's how we hammer the endpoints from a user's seat instead of from a unit test.
