for anyonesigned terrain you can fly throughno prior knowledge assumed

Every point you are about to orbit is one signed measurement. Not a rendering of a place and not a photograph: a cloud of individual facts, each recorded at a fixed address on Earth, each carrying the signature of whoever recorded it.

Click any point and you get the fact behind it: the value, the band it belongs to, and the content address you can hand to someone else to check independently. The satellite basemap underneath is reference only and not signed data, which is why it says so rather than blending in.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a point to read it. Verify one yourself or see how the memory is built.
