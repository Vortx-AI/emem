The external memory
of the world.

One record for every place and every fact. Read the same by everyone. Checkable by anyone. Owned by no one. Two minds can agree on the same fact instead of two versions of it.

As more thinking happens outside human heads, the record of what is real can’t sit inside any one of them. So it sits here — in the open, where anyone can read it and everyone can check it.
