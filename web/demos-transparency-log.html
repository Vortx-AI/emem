The log only grows

Every attestation and every memory write is a leaf in one append-only Merkle tree, and emem signs its head. Pin a head, prove an entry sits under it, then prove a later head still contains everything the first one did.

writes
none; every call is a read
checked
in this tab: BLAKE3, Ed25519, RFC 6962 folds
tree
leaf = blake3(0x00 || entry), node = blake3(0x01 || l || r)

What you will see

  • A signed tree head checked against emem.dev's published key.
  • One entry's audit path folded up to that head's root.
  • A consistency proof that a newer head extends the one you pinned.
  • Another operator's co-signature over a head, checked here.
1pin a head

A signed tree head

The head names the tree's size and root at one moment, signed by the responder. Keep it. Everything below is checked against the head you pinned, not against whatever the server says later.


      

    

  


  

    
2inclusion

    

      
One entry is under that head

      
The audit path is one sibling hash per level. Fold them from the entry upward and you must land exactly on the pinned root. About twenty hashes cover more than two million entries.

      

      

    

  


  

    
3consistency

    

      
A later head extends the pinned one

      
The page proves the old tree is a prefix of the new one. It uses a head this browser pinned on an earlier visit, or waits for a newer head. A rewritten entry anywhere in the old tree would break the proof.

      

      

    

  


  

    
4witnesses

    

      
Someone else saw the same history

      
Consistency proofs stop one responder rewriting its past. They cannot stop it showing two people two different logs. For that, independent witnesses co-sign heads they saw, and anyone can check the current head extends theirs.

      

      

    

  


  

    
What this does not show

    
    
      
  • That any fact in the log is true. The log proves the history was not rewritten, nothing about the measurements in it.
    • 
      
  • The receipt's own merkle_proof. That binds a fact into its signing batch and is checked by the receipt verifier, not by this tree.
    • 
      
  • A split view you never compare. Pin heads, share them with other agents, and check consistency between what each of you saw.
    • 
      
  • A witness vouches only for the prefix it signed. Read entries_behind_current before leaning on it.
    •
